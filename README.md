## What it does

One desktop app for the picture, the sound, and the file.

**Video.** [Upscale toward 4K](https://kwaflux.com/features/video-upscaler), [restore old footage](https://kwaflux.com/features/restore-old-video), [grade color and move SDR toward HDR](https://kwaflux.com/features/video-enhancer), [steady a shaky shot](https://kwaflux.com/features/video-stabilization), [raise the frame rate](https://kwaflux.com/features/video-frame-interpolation), and [cut a subject out of the background](https://kwaflux.com/features/video-background-removal).

**Photos and faces.** [Enhance a still](https://kwaflux.com/features/image-enhancer) and [retouch a face](https://kwaflux.com/features/video-beauty-retouch).

**Sound and files.** [Denoise audio](https://kwaflux.com/features/audio-enhancer), [repair a damaged video, image, or audio file](https://kwaflux.com/features/video-file-repair), [convert](https://kwaflux.com/features/video-converter), [compress](https://kwaflux.com/features/video-compressor), and [save a file from a link](https://kwaflux.com/features/file-download).

## Systems

- Windows 10 (64-bit, version 1809 or later) or Windows 11. An Intel, AMD, or NVIDIA GPU from 2016 or later, integrated or discrete.
- macOS 12 or newer, Apple Silicon only.
- 8 GB of memory minimum, 16 GB recommended.
